The Bradley-Terry / Elo Method

This method combines the Elo rating system with the Bradley-Terry model for estimating win probabilities based on paired comparisons.

The expected probability of team A beating team B is calculated using the logistic curve:

$$ P(A > B) = \frac{1}{1 + 10^{(R_B - R_A) / 400}} $$

After each game, ratings are updated based on the actual outcome (\( S_A = 1 \) for a win, \( 0.5 \) for a tie, \( 0 \) for a loss) compared to the expected outcome, scaled by a K-factor (\( K \)):

$$ R_A' = R_A + K \times (S_A - P(A > B)) $$

This implementation also applies a Margin of Victory Multiplier (MoVM), which rewards larger goal differentials up to a maximum cap of 5 goals, scaling down the reward if the heavily favored team wins.

Strength of Schedule (SOS) represents the average pre-game rating of a team's opponents across the season.
