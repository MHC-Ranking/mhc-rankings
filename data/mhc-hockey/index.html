MHC Rankings Explorations

Comparing Rating Algorithms

Overview

This site compiles various ranking algorithm explorations for the MHC committee. We compare our traditional Colley Matrix method with different configurations of the Bradley-Terry Elo (BT-Elo) system to determine the most accurate reflection of team performance over the season.

📝 Read the full Description of Algorithms here

Algorithm Configurations

1. Colley Matrix

A bias-free, mathematically robust ranking system that distributes ratings by solving a system of linear equations based on wins, losses, and opponent strength. It does not factor in goal differential.

  • 📊 League Rankings Report
  • 🏒 Team-by-Team Reports

2. BT-Elo (Standard)

Combines the Elo rating system with the Bradley-Terry model to estimate win probabilities based on paired comparisons. Ratings are iteratively updated based strictly on game outcomes (Win/Loss/Tie).

  • 📊 League Rankings Report
  • 🏒 Team-by-Team Reports

3. BT-Elo with MoVM

Enhances the BT-Elo method by applying a Margin of Victory Multiplier (MoVM). This rewards larger goal differentials (capped at 5 goals), but scales down the reward if a heavily favored team wins.

  • 📊 League Rankings Report
  • 🏒 Team-by-Team Reports

4. Reversed Schedule BT-Elo with MoVM

Since the Elo algorithm is order-dependent, this configuration processes the exact same games in reverse chronological order. This helps us understand how much recency bias affects the final ratings.

  • 🏒 Team-by-Team Reports
